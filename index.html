nano-kallimachos

This tool generates LaTeX \nocite commands for Overleaf.
All processing is performed locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

  1. Recompile si.tex in Overleaf to generate si.aux.
  2. Download the generated si.aux file.
  3. Select or drag and drop si.aux below.
  4. Generate si_nocite.tex.
Next step
  • Upload and overwrite si_nocite.tex in Overleaf.
  • Add \input{si_nocite} to main.tex.
